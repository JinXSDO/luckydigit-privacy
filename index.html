Lucky Digit – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

In short: Lucky Digit is an offline arcade card game. You don't need an account. Your progress is saved only on your own device and is not sent to us. There is no real money in the game: all coins, Royal Digits (RD) and prizes are virtual, have no cash value and can never be cashed out.

This policy explains what information the Lucky Digit mobile game (“the game”, “we”, “us”), made by NextFrame, uses and why.

1. Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves a small file in the app's private storage on your phone. It contains:

  • the player name you choose (it can be anything – please don't use your real full name);
  • game progress: virtual coins, Royal Digits, cups, level, characters, cosmetics, achievements, daily rewards and leaderboard position;
  • settings: language, sound volume, vibration and reminder on/off;
  • technical values the game needs, such as the date of your last daily reward.

This file stays on your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your real name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos or location. There is no sign-in and no chat. The weekly leaderboard rivals are computer players – your name is not shared with other people.

3. Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads (for example, to unlock a bonus spin on the Lucky Wheel). You only see an ad when you choose to watch one.

Ads are provided by Unity Ads / Unity LevelPlay (LevelPlay may fill an ad slot through its partner ad networks). To show and measure ads, the ad provider may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, device type, operating system, IP address and general ad interactions. This is done by the ad provider under its own privacy policy:

  • Unity privacy policy
  • Unity game player and app user privacy policy

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time, and you can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game sets local reminders on your phone (for example “your free spin is ready”). They are created by the game on your device – no server and no personal data are involved. You can turn them off in the game (Settings → Reminders) or in your phone's settings.

6. Third-party services

The game is built with the Unity engine (Unity privacy policy) and is distributed through Google Play (Google privacy policy). These services may process technical information under their own policies.

7. Virtual items and no gambling

Lucky Digit is a game for fun. Coins, Royal Digits, packs and prizes are virtual. They cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for real money or anything of real value, and nothing in the game can be cashed out.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game contains simulated card-game betting with virtual money only.

9. Your choices and data deletion

  • All game data is on your device – uninstalling the game removes it.
  • You can turn off reminders and vibration in the game settings.
  • For ad data, use your phone's ad settings or the ad provider's tools linked above.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes (for example if new features are added). The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
